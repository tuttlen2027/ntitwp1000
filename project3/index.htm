Welcome to the Online Pet Photo Club

Welcome to the Online Pet Photo Club! If you share our love of pets and photography, this website is tailor-made for you. We welcome every shutterbug from amateur to professional. Share the smiles that your pet has brought you in a family-friendly online space.


You can also participate in some friendly pet photo competitions to win prizes. We host monthly themed pet photo contests, divided into categories for dogs, cats, birds and small animals. Those age 18 and up are welcome to enter. Residents of every U.S. state are welcome to submit one family-friendly photo of their pets in the contests.


Before you make a submission, please carefully read through all Rules and criteria. The contest each month opens on the first day of the month at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST) and concludes on the last day of the month at 11:59 p.m. EST. Be sure to do Voting on your favorite each month.

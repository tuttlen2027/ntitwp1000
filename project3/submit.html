Welcome to the Online Pet Photography Club Submit!

Please review our Rules page before submitting. Photos for the Online Pet Photography Club will not be accepted outside of this Submit form.

Please enter your first and last name, email and phone number.

In the message section, please include some information about your photo. This should include the name and age of your pet, the state and city in which the photo was taken, and the date of the photo.

If you are entering a contest, please also enter in the message section the theme name, as well as the full opening and closing dates of the contest you wish to enter. Please additionally indicate which category of the contest you wish to enter in the message section. For March, our theme is St. Patrick’s Day. March contest entries are accepted March 1-31, 2026.

If you simply want to submit a photo for consideration to be featured on our site, please indicate this in the message section along with the information regarding type of pet, name of pet, age of pet, etc.

Whether or not you wish to enter a contest, please fill out the category checkbox section. Please note that we have created a new and separate category for our Feathered Friends, pet birds.

You will be notified via email within 24 hours if your photo has been accepted. Contest winners are selected via voting on our website. If you are a contest winner, you will be notified via email on the first day of the new month after the contest due date.

Voting

The images pictured here are those submitted thus far for the March 2026 Online Pet Photo Club contest theme of St. Patrick’s Day: Wearing of the Green. The March photo competition is open March 1-31, 2026. Please view our Rules and Submit pages for photo submission requirements. The Categories page is also helpful. If you are a contest winner, you will be notified via email on the first day of the new month after the contest due date. This month’s prizes are sponsored by PetSmart and will be based upon the type of pet you have pictured.

For those of you ready to vote, take a good look at our March 2026 Online Pet Photo Club St. Patrick’s Day submissions thus far. The voting public may vote only once per category, so take your time to examine each photo carefully. Please vote by clicking on the button below your favorite photo in each category. Those who enter the contest are not permitted to vote for their own photo. Solicitations for votes are not permitted. For those of you looking ahead, our April 2026 Online Pet Photo Club theme will be Easter/Springtime, so get hopping to get those adorable images of your pets ready to send by April 1- no fooling!

Gallery

Welcome to the Online Pet Photography Club photo gallery! This photo gallery currently displays our winners in the February Online Pet Photography Club photo contest. The February 2026 contest was held February 1-28, 2026. The February 2026 theme was Valentine’s Day: How Do You Show Your Love for Pets? Photos could depict you showing your pet some love, or your pet doing something that they love (such as a game of fetch). Pictured are the top winners in the categories of Dogs, Cats and Small Animals. (Remember, as of February we had not yet added the Birds category. This is new for the March competition!) Winners are depicted on the website and received prizes courtesy of Pet Supplies Plus. Prizes varied depending on the type of pet each winner had.

Are you interested in participating in our Online Pet Photography Club March photo competition? Take a good look at our Rules and Submit pages for photo submission requirements. Our Categories page also offers some helpful hints for pet-loving photographers. Our theme for March 2026 is St. Patrick’s Day, with pets wearing green or depicted in green settings. Winners are selected by our website viewing and voting audience. Our viewers have told us they often judge on cuteness, cuddliness and uniqueness, so be creative, inventive, safe, respectful and loving in your pet photos!
